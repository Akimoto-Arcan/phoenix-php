Getting Started with Phoenix

Phoenix is a tool that helps your business manage sales, inventory, maintenance, production, and scheduling all in one place. Think of it as a central hub where everyone on your team can find what they need.

What You'll See After Logging In

When you first log in, you'll see the Dashboard — a summary page showing your installed modules, system status, and key information. On the left side is the sidebar — this is how you navigate to different parts of the system.

Your Modules

  1. Point of Sale — Ring up sales, accept payments (cash or card), create invoices, and manage purchase orders.
  2. Inventory — Track what you have in stock, get alerts when supplies are low, and manage suppliers.
  3. Maintenance (CMMS) — Create work orders, schedule preventive maintenance, and track equipment.
  4. Production — Monitor production lines, log runs, track downtime and defects.
  5. Scheduling — View shift schedules, request time off, and swap shifts with coworkers.
  6. Chat — Communicate with your team in organized channels.
  7. Reports — Run and create reports to analyze your business data.
Tip: Click any module in the sidebar to get started. Each module has its own help section — look for the 📖 Help link at the bottom of the sidebar.
