# Maintenance Parts Inventory

## What Is Parts Inventory?

**Parts inventory** is a dedicated stock of spare parts and maintenance supplies. These are items the maintenance team uses to repair and maintain equipment — things like belts, bearings, filters, lubricants, fuses, and seals.

This is separate from the main product inventory. Parts inventory is specifically for maintenance use.

## Adding Parts

1. Click **Maintenance** in the main menu.
2. Click **Parts**.
3. Click the **Add Part** button.
4. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Part Number** — A unique identifier for the part (for example, "BRG-6205" for a specific bearing).
   - **Name** — A descriptive name (for example, "6205 Ball Bearing").
   - **Quantity** — How many you currently have in stock.
   - **Reorder Point** — The minimum quantity before you need to order more. When stock falls to this number or below, the part is flagged as low stock.
   - **Cost** — The price per unit.
   - **Location** — Where the part is stored (for example, "Parts Room, Bin C-14").
5. Click **Save**.

> Tip: Use consistent naming for part numbers. A system like "BRG" for bearings, "BLT" for belts, and "FLT" for filters makes parts easier to find.

## Low Stock Alerts for Parts

Parts with a **reorder point** set are automatically monitored. When the quantity on hand drops to the reorder point or below, the part appears with a **low stock warning** on the Parts page.

Check the Parts page regularly to catch low stock items before you run out. Running out of a critical spare part during an emergency repair causes costly delays.

> Warning: Parts with no reorder point set will never trigger a low stock alert, even if the quantity reaches zero. Set a reorder point for every critical part.

## Using Parts on a Work Order

When a technician uses a part to complete a repair, they should record it on the work order. This keeps your parts inventory accurate and creates a record of what was used.

1. Open the **work order** where the part was used.
2. Scroll to the **Parts Used** section.
3. Click **Add Part**.
4. Search for and select the part.
5. Enter the **quantity** used.
6. Click **Save**.

The part's stock count is automatically reduced by the quantity entered.

> Tip: Recording parts usage on work orders helps you predict future needs. If you see that a certain bearing is used every three months, you know to keep extras in stock.
