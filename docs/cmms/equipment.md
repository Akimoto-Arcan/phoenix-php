# Managing Equipment

## What Is the Equipment Registry?

The **equipment registry** is a master list of every machine, asset, and piece of equipment your maintenance team is responsible for. Each entry contains identifying details, its current condition, and a history of all maintenance performed on it.

Think of it as a profile page for each machine. Anyone on the team can look up a piece of equipment and see its specifications, current status, and past work orders.

## Adding New Equipment

1. Click **Maintenance** in the main menu.
2. Click **Equipment**.
3. Click the **Add Equipment** button.
4. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Name** — A clear, recognizable name for the equipment (for example, "UD Line 3 Winder").
   - **Asset Tag** — A unique identifier, often a number printed on a label attached to the physical machine.
   - **Category** — The type of equipment (for example, "Production Machine," "HVAC," "Conveyor").
   - **Location** — Where the equipment is physically located (for example, "Building A, Bay 2").
   - **Manufacturer** — The company that made the equipment.
   - **Model** — The manufacturer's model name or number.
   - **Serial Number** — The unique serial number found on the equipment's nameplate.
5. Click **Save**.

> Tip: Take a photo of the equipment nameplate before adding it. The nameplate usually has the manufacturer, model, and serial number all in one place.

## Equipment Status

Each piece of equipment has a status that tells everyone its current condition:

- **Operational** — The equipment is running normally. No issues.
- **Maintenance** — The equipment is undergoing scheduled or unscheduled maintenance. It may be partially available.
- **Down** — The equipment is not working and cannot be used. A work order should be open for it.
- **Retired** — The equipment has been permanently taken out of service.

To change a piece of equipment's status:

1. Go to **Equipment** and click on the machine.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Change the **Status** field.
4. Click **Save**.

> Warning: When you set equipment to **Down**, make sure a work order exists so the maintenance team knows about the problem. Setting it to Down without a work order means no one may follow up.

## Viewing Equipment History

Each equipment profile includes a history of all maintenance performed on it.

1. Go to **Equipment** and click on the machine.
2. Scroll down to the **Work Order History** section.

This section lists every work order — open, completed, and cancelled — linked to that piece of equipment. It shows dates, descriptions, who did the work, and how it was resolved.

Use this history to spot patterns. If a machine has repeated failures of the same type, it may need a bigger repair, a replacement part, or a change to its preventive maintenance schedule.
