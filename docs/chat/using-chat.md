# Using Chat

This guide covers the basics of using the Chat module: joining channels, sending messages, getting someone's attention, and finding old conversations.

## Joining a Channel

1. Go to **Chat** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Browse Channels** to see a list of available channels.
3. Find the channel you want to join and click **Join**.

Once you join a channel, it appears in your channel list on the left side of the screen. You will see new messages as they are posted.

> Tip: You may be automatically added to certain channels based on your team or role. Check your channel list when you first log in.

## Sending a Message

1. Click on a **channel name** in the left sidebar to open it.
2. Type your message in the **text box** at the bottom of the screen.
3. Press **Enter** on your keyboard or click the **Send** button.

Your message appears in the channel immediately and is visible to everyone in that channel. Keep messages clear and concise.

## @Mentions

If you need to get a specific person's attention, use an **@mention**. Type the **@** symbol followed by their name (for example, **@John Smith**). The system will show a list of matching names as you type — click the correct name to select it.

When you @mention someone, they receive a notification even if they are not currently looking at the channel. Use @mentions when you need a response from a specific person.

## Presence Indicators

Next to each person's name, you will see a small colored dot that shows whether they are currently available:

- **Green dot** — The person is **online** and currently using the system.
- **Yellow dot** — The person is **away**. They are logged in but have not been active for a few minutes.
- **Gray dot** — The person is **offline** and not currently logged in.

These indicators help you know whether someone is likely to respond quickly.

## Finding Old Messages

If you need to find a message from an earlier conversation:

1. Click the **Search** icon at the top of the Chat module.
2. Type a keyword or phrase from the message you are looking for.
3. Press **Enter** to search.
4. The results will show matching messages across all channels you belong to.

Click on any result to jump to that message in its original channel.

> Tip: If you know which channel the message was in, open that channel first and scroll up to browse through the conversation history.
