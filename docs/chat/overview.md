# Chat — Overview

The **Chat** module is a built-in messaging system for communicating with your coworkers during work. You can send messages to individuals or groups without needing to use personal phones or outside apps.

## Channels

Conversations in the Chat module are organized into **channels**. A channel is a group conversation focused on a specific topic, team, or project. For example, there might be a channel for your production line, a channel for maintenance requests, or a general announcements channel.

Channels keep conversations organized so you can find information quickly. Instead of scrolling through one long list of messages, you go to the channel that matches what you need.

## How Chat Is Different from Text Messaging

The Chat module is designed for **work communication**, which makes it different from personal text messaging in a few important ways:

- Messages are visible to everyone in the channel, so the whole team stays informed.
- Conversations are organized by topic, not by person, which makes it easier to find information later.
- Your employer can view chat messages, so keep all communication professional and work-related.

> Tip: Use chat for quick work questions, shift updates, and team coordination. For personal conversations, use your personal phone during breaks.
