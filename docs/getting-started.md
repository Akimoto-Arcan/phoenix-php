# Getting Started with Phoenix

## What is Phoenix?

**Phoenix** is a tool that helps your business manage sales, inventory, maintenance, production, and scheduling all in one place. Instead of using separate programs for each part of your business, Phoenix puts everything into a single system that your whole team can access from any web browser.

Think of it as your company's central hub. Everyone logs in, sees the tools they need, and gets their work done without switching between different apps.

## Logging In for the First Time

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari all work).
2. Go to the address your administrator gave you (for example, `http://192.168.1.186`).
3. Type your **Username** in the first field.
4. Type your **Password** in the second field.
5. Click the **Sign In** button.

> Tip: If you do not have an account yet, click the **Register** link below the sign-in form. Your administrator will need to approve your account before you can log in.

## What You See After Logging In

When you sign in, you land on the **Dashboard**. This is your home screen. It shows:

- **Welcome message** with your name and today's date.
- **Installed Modules** -- a count of how many tools are available in your system.
- **Your Role** -- the permission level your administrator assigned to you (for example, Operator, Supervisor, or Admin).
- **System Status** -- a quick health check showing that Phoenix is running normally.
- **Installed Modules list** -- the names and version numbers of every module that has been set up.

## Finding What You Need

The **sidebar** on the left side of the screen is your main menu. It is always visible on desktop screens and can be opened with the menu button on phones and tablets.

The sidebar is divided into sections:

- **Main** -- takes you back to the Dashboard.
- **Modules** -- lists every tool available to you, such as Point of Sale, Inventory, or Maintenance.
- **Administration** -- visible only to Admins. Contains user management, roles, and system settings.
- **Help & Docs** -- opens documentation and help guides.

Click any item in the sidebar to go to that section. The active page is highlighted so you always know where you are.

## The Seven Modules

Phoenix includes seven modules. Your administrator chooses which ones to install based on what your business needs.

**Point of Sale (POS)** -- Process sales, accept cash and card payments, manage customers, create invoices, and handle purchase orders. Stock is automatically deducted from inventory when you make a sale.

**Inventory Management** -- Track every item your business owns. Manage stock levels, receive shipments, set reorder points, and get alerts when supplies run low.

**CMMS (Maintenance Management)** -- Create and track work orders for equipment maintenance. Schedule preventive maintenance, manage parts, and assign technicians.

**Production Tracker** -- Monitor production output across machines and lines. Track operator shifts, quality metrics, and efficiency in real time.

**Shift Scheduling** -- Build employee schedules with a drag-and-drop calendar. Handle shift swaps, availability, and overtime tracking.

**Report Builder** -- Create custom reports from any module's data. Export to PDF or Excel, schedule automatic reports, and build visual dashboards.

**Internal Chat** -- Send messages to your team through channels and direct messages. Share files and stay coordinated without leaving Phoenix.

> Tip: You only see modules that your administrator has installed and that your role has permission to access. If a module is missing from your sidebar, ask your administrator about access.
