# Navigating the Dashboard

## The Sidebar

The **sidebar** is the vertical menu on the left side of the screen. It is your primary way to move around Phoenix.

The sidebar is organized into sections:

### Main

This section contains the **Dashboard** link. Click it from anywhere in the system to return to your home screen.

### Modules

This section lists every tool that has been installed and that you have permission to use. Common entries include:

- **Point of Sale** -- sales register, orders, invoices, and customers.
- **Inventory** -- stock tracking, receiving, and fulfillment.
- **Maintenance** -- work orders, assets, and preventive maintenance.
- **Production** -- production tracking, shift reports, and efficiency.
- **Scheduling** -- employee shift calendars and availability.
- **Reports** -- custom reports and dashboards.
- **Chat** -- internal team messaging.

### Administration

This section appears only for users with **Admin** or **SuperAdmin** roles. It contains:

- **Users** -- add, edit, or deactivate user accounts.
- **Roles & Permissions** -- control who can access what.
- **Settings** -- system-wide configuration options.

### Help & Docs

Click **Help & Docs** at the bottom of the sidebar to open documentation and user guides.

## Switching Between Modules

1. Look at the **sidebar** on the left.
2. Find the module you want under the **Modules** section.
3. Click its name.

The page will load and the sidebar item will highlight to show where you are. Each module has its own set of tabs or pages inside it.

> Tip: The sidebar remembers which page you are on by highlighting it. If you ever feel lost, look at the sidebar to see which item is active.

## The Topbar

The **topbar** is the horizontal bar at the very top of the screen. It shows:

- **Page title** -- the name of the page you are currently viewing (for example, "Dashboard" or "Point of Sale").
- **Theme toggle button** -- a sun or moon icon. Click it to switch between **light mode** and **dark mode**. Your preference is remembered.
- **Your username** -- displayed on the right side so you can confirm which account is logged in.

## Switching Between Light and Dark Mode

1. Look at the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Click the **sun** or **moon** icon.
3. The screen will immediately switch themes.

Dark mode uses a dark background with light text. Light mode uses a white background with dark text. Choose whichever is easier on your eyes.

## Mobile Devices

On phones and tablets, the sidebar is hidden by default to save screen space.

1. Tap the **hamburger menu** button (three horizontal lines) in the top-left corner.
2. The sidebar will slide in from the left.
3. Tap the page you want to visit.
4. The sidebar will close automatically after you make a selection.

> Tip: Phoenix works on any modern mobile browser. If the screen feels cramped, try rotating your device to landscape orientation.

## Getting Back to the Main Dashboard

There are two ways to return to the main dashboard from anywhere:

1. Click **Dashboard** in the sidebar under the **Main** section.
2. Click the **Phoenix logo** at the top of the sidebar.

Both will take you back to the home screen where you can see your system overview and module list.

## Signing Out

1. Scroll to the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Click **Sign Out** (shown in red).
3. You will be returned to the login screen.

> Warning: Always sign out when you leave a shared or public computer. If you walk away without signing out, your session will eventually expire on its own, but it is safer to log out manually.
