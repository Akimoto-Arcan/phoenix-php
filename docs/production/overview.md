# Production Tracking — Overview

The **Production** module is where you track what your production lines are making, how efficiently they are running, and any problems that come up during a shift. Think of it as a digital logbook for everything happening on the factory floor.

## What You Can Do

The Production module has five main areas:

- **Dashboard** — A live summary of all your production lines, showing what is running right now and how things are going.
- **Lines** — The list of your production lines, their current status, and their setup details.
- **Runs** — Individual batches of production. Each time a line starts making a product, that is a run.
- **Downtime** — A log of every time a line stops, why it stopped, and how long it was down.
- **Defects** — A record of any quality issues found during production.

## Key Metrics

The system tracks four numbers that tell you how your production is performing:

- **Output** — The total number of good units produced. This counts only units that passed quality checks.
- **Efficiency** — How much of your available production time was actually spent making good product. A higher percentage is better.
- **Downtime** — The total time your lines were not producing. The system breaks this down by category so you can see what causes the most lost time.
- **Defect Rate** — The percentage of units that had quality problems. A lower number is better.

> Tip: Check the Dashboard at the start of your shift to get a quick picture of how things are going across all lines.
