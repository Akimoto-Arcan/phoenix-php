# Your Account

## Logging In

1. Open your web browser and go to the Phoenix address (your administrator will provide this).
2. Enter your **Username** in the first field.
3. Enter your **Password** in the second field.
4. Click **Sign In**.

If your credentials are correct, you will be taken to the Dashboard.

## What to Do If You Cannot Log In

There are several reasons you might not be able to sign in. Here is what to check:

### "Invalid username or password"

You entered the wrong username or password. Double-check your spelling, make sure Caps Lock is off, and try again.

> Warning: After 5 failed login attempts, your account will be temporarily locked for 15 minutes. Wait and try again, or contact your administrator to unlock it.

### "Your account is awaiting admin approval"

You recently registered but an administrator has not approved your account yet. Contact your administrator and ask them to approve your registration.

### "Account is disabled"

An administrator has deactivated your account. Contact your administrator to find out why and request reactivation if needed.

### "Account is temporarily locked"

You entered the wrong password too many times. Wait 15 minutes and try again. The lock will clear automatically.

### "Mobile access is restricted"

You are trying to log in from a phone or tablet, but your account has not been approved for mobile use. Contact your administrator to request mobile access.

## Session Timeout

A **session** is the period of time between when you log in and when the system logs you out. Phoenix tracks your activity to keep the system secure.

For most users (Operators, Inspectors, Lab users), sessions last 12 hours. This means you can stay logged in for a full work shift without being interrupted.

For users with higher-level access (Supervisors and Admins), sessions are shorter -- typically 10 minutes of inactivity. This is a security measure to protect sensitive functions.

When your session expires, you will see the login screen the next time you try to do something. Simply log in again to continue.

> Tip: If you find yourself getting logged out frequently, it may be because your role has a shorter timeout. Your administrator can adjust your individual session timeout if needed.

## Changing Your Theme Preference

Phoenix supports two visual themes:

- **Dark mode** -- dark background with light text. Easier on the eyes in low-light environments.
- **Light mode** -- white background with dark text. Better for brightly lit rooms.

To switch themes:

1. Look at the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Click the **sun** or **moon** icon in the topbar.
3. The theme changes instantly.

Your theme preference is saved automatically.

## Registering a New Account

If you need an account and your organization allows self-registration:

1. Go to the Phoenix login page.
2. Click the **Register** link below the sign-in form.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Username** -- choose a unique name you will use to log in.
   - **Full Name** -- your real name so your team can identify you.
   - **Email** -- optional but recommended.
   - **Password** -- must be at least 8 characters long.
   - **Confirm Password** -- type the same password again.
4. Click **Create Account**.

After registering, your account must be approved by an administrator before you can log in. You will see a message confirming that your account is awaiting approval.

> Tip: If you already have an account and need to return to the login page, click the **Sign In** link below the registration form.

## Account Roles

Your administrator assigns you a **role** that controls what you can see and do in Phoenix. Common roles include:

- **Operator** -- can access production tools and the POS register.
- **Inspection** -- can access quality control tools and reports.
- **Lab** -- can access lab-related inspection and testing tools.
- **Maintenance** -- can access the CMMS module for work orders and parts.
- **Supervisor** -- can access most modules plus management tools.
- **Admin** -- full access to all modules, user management, and settings.
- **SuperAdmin** -- unrestricted access to everything in the system.

If you need access to a module or feature that you cannot see, contact your administrator to request a role change.
