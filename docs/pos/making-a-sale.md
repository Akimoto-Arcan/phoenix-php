# Making a Sale

## The Sales Process at a Glance

The basic flow for every sale is the same: find the product, add it to the cart, and process the payment. Here is the full process from start to finish.

## Step 1: Find the Product

There are three ways to add products to the cart:

### Typing in the Search Bar

1. Click the **search bar** at the top of the Register screen. It says "Scan barcode or search by SKU / name..."
2. Start typing the product name, SKU code, or UPC barcode number.
3. Matching products will appear as cards below the search bar.
4. Click the product card to add it to your cart.

### Scanning a Barcode

1. Make sure your cursor is in the **search bar** (click it if it is not).
2. Point your barcode scanner at the product's barcode and scan it.
3. The scanner types the barcode number into the search bar automatically.
4. If the barcode matches a product, it is added to the cart instantly.
5. The search bar clears itself so you can scan the next item right away.

### Clicking Product Cards

When you search, products appear as clickable cards showing the name, SKU, price, and stock count. Simply click any card to add that product to the cart.

> Tip: Products that are out of stock appear grayed out and cannot be added to the cart. If you need to sell an out-of-stock item, ask your inventory manager to update the stock count first.

## Step 2: Review the Cart

The **cart** is on the right side of the screen. It shows every item you have added along with:

- **Product name** and SKU.
- **Unit price** -- the price of one item.
- **Quantity** -- how many of that item are being sold.
- **Line total** -- the price multiplied by the quantity.

### Changing the Quantity

- Click the **+** button next to an item to add one more.
- Click the **-** button to remove one.
- You can also type a number directly into the **quantity box** between the + and - buttons.

### Removing an Item

Click the **X** button on the far right of any cart item to remove it completely.

### Clearing the Entire Cart

Click the **Clear** button at the bottom of the cart to remove all items and start over.

## Step 3: Review the Totals

Below the cart items, you will see a breakdown of the transaction:

- **Subtotal** -- the total price of all items before tax and discounts.
- **Tax** -- the tax amount, calculated automatically based on your configured tax rate.
- **Discount** -- you can type a dollar amount here to apply a flat discount to the sale.
- **Total** -- the final amount the customer owes (subtotal + tax - discount).

## Step 4: Choose a Payment Method

At the bottom of the cart, you will see four buttons:

- **Cash** -- the customer is paying with cash. See [Processing Cash Payments](cash-payments.md).
- **Card** -- the customer is paying with a credit or debit card. See [Processing Card Payments](card-payments.md).
- **Invoice** -- create an invoice for the customer to pay later. See [Creating Invoices](invoicing.md).
- **Clear** -- cancel the sale and empty the cart.

Click the appropriate payment button to proceed.

## What Happens After Payment

Once a payment is confirmed:

1. A **receipt** appears on screen showing the order number, items, totals, and payment details.
2. The **cart is cleared** and ready for the next customer.
3. The sold items are **deducted from inventory** automatically.
4. The sale is **recorded** in the Orders tab and linked to your register session.

> Tip: If the customer needs a printed receipt, your system may be configured to print automatically. Ask your supervisor how receipts are handled at your location.
