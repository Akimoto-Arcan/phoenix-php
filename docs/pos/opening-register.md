# Opening the Register

## What Is a Register Session?

A **register session** is a time period during which you are responsible for a specific cash register. It starts when you open the register at the beginning of your shift and ends when you close it.

Opening a session tells the system who is using the register and how much cash is in the drawer at the start. This lets Phoenix track all sales and calculate whether your cash count matches at the end of the day.

## Step by Step: Opening a Session

1. Click **Point of Sale** in the sidebar to open the POS module.
2. Click the **Register** tab (this is usually the default view when you open POS).
3. The system will check whether you already have an open session. If you do not, a prompt will appear asking you to open one.
4. Select the **register** you are assigned to (if your location has more than one).
5. Enter your **Starting Cash** amount. This is the amount of cash already in the drawer when you begin (for example, $100.00).
6. Click **Open Session**.

The register is now active and ready for sales.

## What "Session Open" Means

Once your session is open, every sale you process is linked to your session. The system keeps a running total of:

- How many orders you have processed.
- How much was paid in cash.
- How much was paid by card.
- Your total sales for the session.

This information is used when you close the register at the end of your shift.

## What If Someone Else Already Has an Open Session?

Each person can only have **one open session at a time**. If you try to open a new session while you already have one open, the system will show an error:

*"You already have an open session. Close it before opening a new one."*

To fix this:

1. Close your existing session first (see the [End of Day guide](end-of-day.md)).
2. Then open a new session.

If a different person left a session open on the register you need to use, ask them to close their session or contact your supervisor for help.

> Tip: Always close your register session at the end of your shift. Leaving a session open makes it harder for the next person to start theirs and can cause confusion in your sales reports.
