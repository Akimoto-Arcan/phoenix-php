# Managing Customers

## Overview

The **Customers** tab in the POS module is your customer directory. You can store contact information, track order history, and manage tax-exempt status for business customers.

## Adding a New Customer

1. Open the **Point of Sale** module.
2. Click the **Customers** tab.
3. Click the **Add Customer** button.
4. Fill in the customer's information:
   - **Name** -- the customer's full name or business name. This is the only required field.
   - **Email** -- the customer's email address (optional).
   - **Phone** -- the customer's phone number (optional).
   - **Company** -- the company or organization name (optional).
   - **Address, City, State, ZIP** -- the customer's mailing address (optional).
   - **Tax Exempt** -- check this box if the customer is exempt from sales tax (see below).
   - **Notes** -- any additional information about the customer (optional).
5. Click **Save**.

The customer will now appear in your customer list and can be linked to sales and invoices.

## Tax-Exempt Customers

Some customers -- such as government agencies, nonprofits, or resellers -- may be exempt from paying sales tax. When a customer is marked as **tax-exempt**, sales made to that customer will not include tax charges.

To set a customer as tax-exempt:

1. Open the customer record (either when creating a new customer or by clicking **Edit** on an existing one).
2. Check the **Tax Exempt** box.
3. Click **Save**.

Tax-exempt customers are marked with an **Exempt** badge in the customer list so they are easy to identify.

> Tip: Keep a copy of the customer's tax-exemption certificate on file. Your business may be required to prove the exemption during an audit.

## Searching for Existing Customers

If you need to find a customer who is already in the system:

1. Go to the **Customers** tab.
2. Type the customer's name, email, phone number, or company name in the **search bar** at the top.
3. The list will filter as you type.
4. Click the customer to view their details or click **Edit** to update their information.

## Editing a Customer

1. Find the customer in the list.
2. Click the **Edit** button on their row.
3. Update the fields that need to change.
4. Click **Save**.

## Linking a Customer to a Sale or Invoice

When processing a sale or creating an invoice, you can link it to a customer:

- **During an invoice** -- when you click the **Invoice** button during a sale, you are required to select a customer from the dropdown list before the invoice can be created.
- **On an existing order** -- you can update the customer field on an open order before payment is processed.

Linking customers to transactions lets you track purchasing history and maintain accurate records for invoicing and account management.

> Tip: If a customer is not in the dropdown when you are creating an invoice, you will need to add them in the Customers tab first. Go to Customers, add the customer, then return to your sale.
