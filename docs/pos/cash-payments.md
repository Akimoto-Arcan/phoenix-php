# Processing Cash Payments

## Overview

Cash payments are the simplest type of transaction. You enter the amount the customer gives you, the system calculates the change, and the sale is complete.

## Step by Step

### Step 1: Add Items to the Cart

Find the products the customer wants to buy and add them to the cart. Review the totals to confirm the correct amount. See [Making a Sale](making-a-sale.md) for detailed instructions.

### Step 2: Click the Cash Button

At the bottom of the cart, click the green **Cash** button. A payment window will appear showing the total amount due.

### Step 3: Enter the Amount the Customer Gives You

In the **Amount Tendered** field, type the amount of cash the customer hands you. For example, if the total is $27.50 and the customer gives you $30.00, type `30.00`.

The field will pre-fill with the total rounded up to the nearest dollar, but you should always update it to match the actual amount received.

### Step 4: Check the Change

The system automatically calculates the **Change Due** and displays it in green below the amount tendered. Double-check that this number is correct.

> Warning: If the amount tendered is less than the total, the system will not let you complete the payment. Make sure the amount entered is equal to or greater than the total due.

### Step 5: Confirm the Payment

Click the **Confirm Payment** button. The system will:

1. Record the payment.
2. Mark the order as paid.
3. Deduct the sold items from inventory.
4. Show you a receipt screen.

## What You See: The Receipt

After the payment is confirmed, a receipt screen appears with:

- **Order number** (for example, POS-2026-0042).
- A list of every item sold, with quantities and prices.
- The **subtotal**, **tax**, **discount** (if any), and **total**.
- The **amount tendered** and the **change due**.

This is your confirmation that the sale was recorded. Click **Done** to close the receipt and return to the register for the next customer.

## Tips for Cash Handling

> Tip: Always count the change before handing it to the customer. Start from the sale total and count up to the amount they gave you.

> Tip: If a customer gives you the exact amount, you can leave the Amount Tendered field at the total. The change due will show $0.00.

> Tip: Place the customer's bill on top of the register (not in the drawer) until you have counted out the change. This prevents disputes about what denomination they gave you.
