# Reports — Overview

The **Reports** module lets you pull up summaries of your production data, scheduling records, and other information. Instead of digging through individual records one at a time, reports give you the big picture in tables, charts, and summary numbers.

## Pre-Built Reports vs Custom Reports

The system comes with two kinds of reports:

- **Pre-built reports** are ready to use out of the box. They cover common needs like daily production summaries, downtime breakdowns, and quality trends. You just pick the report, set a date range, and run it.
- **Custom reports** are reports that an administrator has created using the Report Builder. These are tailored to your facility's specific needs and can pull from any data in the system.

Both types of reports work the same way once they are set up. The only difference is who created them.

## Who Can Run Reports

Your ability to run reports depends on your **role** and **permissions**:

- **Operators** can typically run reports related to their own production line.
- **Supervisors** can run reports for their team and their lines.
- **Administrators** and **SuperAdmins** can run any report in the system and create new custom reports.

If you cannot find a report you need, ask your supervisor or administrator. They may be able to give you access or run the report for you.

> Tip: Reports are a great way to prepare for shift meetings. Run a daily production summary before your meeting to have the numbers ready.
