# Running Reports

Running a report means asking the system to gather data and show you the results. This guide walks you through finding a report, running it, understanding the output, and exporting the results.

## How to Run a Report

1. Go to **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Browse the list of available reports or use the **Search** box to find one by name.
3. Click on the report you want to run.
4. Set the **parameters** — most reports ask you to choose a date range, a production line, or other filters. Fill in the fields that appear.
5. Click **Run Report**.
6. Wait a moment while the system gathers the data. Larger reports may take a few seconds.

The results appear on your screen once the report is finished.

## Understanding the Output

Reports can show information in several formats:

- **Tables** — Rows and columns of data, similar to a spreadsheet. You can usually click on column headers to sort the data.
- **Charts** — Visual graphs like bar charts, line charts, or pie charts that make trends easier to spot.
- **Summary numbers** — Key totals or averages displayed prominently at the top of the report, such as total output, average efficiency, or total downtime hours.

Most reports combine all three formats so you get both the details and the big picture.

## Exporting Reports

If you need to save a report or share it with someone who does not have system access:

1. After running the report, look for the **Export** button (usually in the top-right corner of the results).
2. Choose your format:
   - **PDF** — Best for printing or emailing a formatted copy.
   - **CSV** — Best for opening in a spreadsheet program like Excel for further analysis.
3. The file will download to your computer.

> Tip: If you run the same report regularly, bookmark it or ask an administrator to schedule it to run automatically and send the results to your email.
