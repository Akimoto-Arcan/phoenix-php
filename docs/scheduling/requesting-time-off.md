# Requesting Time Off

When you need to take a day off work, you submit a time off request through the system. Your manager will review the request and approve or deny it. You can check the status of your request at any time.

## How to Submit a Request

1. Go to **Scheduling** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Time Off** tab.
3. Click **Request Time Off**.
4. Select your **Start Date** and **End Date** using the date pickers. For a single day off, set both dates to the same day.
5. Choose the **Type** of time off from the drop-down menu (see types below).
6. Enter a **Reason** for the request. Keep it brief but clear.
7. Click **Submit**.

Your request is now sent to your manager for review.

## Time Off Types

When submitting a request, choose the type that matches your situation:

- **Vacation** — Planned time off for personal rest or travel.
- **Sick** — Time off due to illness or medical appointments.
- **Personal** — Time off for personal matters that do not fit other categories.
- **Family** — Time off to handle family obligations, such as caring for a family member.
- **Bereavement** — Time off following the death of a family member or close friend.
- **Jury Duty** — Time off to fulfill jury duty or other court obligations.

> Tip: Submit vacation requests as early as possible. Requests submitted well in advance are more likely to be approved, especially during busy periods.

## What Happens After You Submit

Once you submit your request, it goes to your manager's approval queue. Your manager will review it and either approve or deny it. You will receive a notification when a decision is made.

You do not need to follow up unless you have not heard back within a few days.

## Checking the Status of Your Request

1. Go to **Scheduling** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Time Off** tab.
3. Your requests are listed with their current status.

Each request will show one of these statuses:

- **Pending** — Your request has been submitted but your manager has not reviewed it yet.
- **Approved** — Your time off has been approved. The days will appear on your schedule.
- **Denied** — Your request was not approved. Your manager may have added a note explaining why. If you have questions, talk to your manager directly.

> Warning: A pending request does not mean your time off is approved. Do not make travel plans or other commitments until your request shows as **Approved**.
