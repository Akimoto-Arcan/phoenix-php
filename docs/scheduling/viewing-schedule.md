# Viewing Your Schedule

Your work schedule shows which shifts you are assigned to work. The system displays your schedule in a weekly calendar view so you can see your upcoming shifts at a glance.

## How to Find Your Shifts

1. Go to **Scheduling** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Calendar** tab.
3. Your shifts for the current week are displayed automatically.
4. Use the **arrow buttons** at the top of the calendar to move forward or backward by one week.

If you are a supervisor or manager, you will see your team's shifts as well. Use the **Employee** drop-down to filter the calendar to a specific person.

## Understanding the Weekly Calendar View

The calendar shows seven days across the top (Monday through Sunday). Each shift appears as a colored block on the day it is scheduled.

Inside each block, you will see:
- The **start time** and **end time** of the shift.
- The **shift type** (for example, Day, Night, or Swing).
- The **location** or line assignment, if applicable.

## Shift Types and Colors

Different shift types are shown in different colors so you can quickly tell them apart:

- **Blue** — Day shift.
- **Purple** — Night shift.
- **Green** — Swing shift (a shift that bridges day and night).
- **Orange** — Overtime or extra shift.
- **Gray** — Off day or unscheduled.

The exact colors may vary depending on how your administrator has set up the system.

## What to Do if Your Schedule Is Wrong

If you see an error in your schedule — for example, you are assigned to a shift you cannot work, or a shift is missing — do the following:

1. Contact your **supervisor** directly. Do not try to change the schedule yourself.
2. Let them know which date and shift is incorrect.
3. Your supervisor can update the schedule or help arrange a shift swap if needed.

> Tip: Do not wait until the last minute to report a schedule problem. The sooner you let your supervisor know, the easier it is to fix.
