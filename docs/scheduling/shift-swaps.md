# Shift Swaps

A **shift swap** is when you trade shifts with a coworker. For example, if you are scheduled for Monday morning and your coworker is scheduled for Tuesday evening, you can swap so each of you works the other's shift. Both the coworker and a manager must agree before the swap takes effect.

## How to Request a Shift Swap

1. Go to **Scheduling** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Shift Swaps** tab.
3. Click **New Swap Request**.
4. Select **Your Shift** — the shift you want to give up.
5. Select the **Coworker** you want to swap with from the drop-down list.
6. Select the **Coworker's Shift** — the shift you want to take from them.
7. Click **Submit Swap Request**.

Your coworker will receive a notification asking them to accept or decline the swap.

> Tip: Talk to your coworker before submitting the swap request. It is better to agree on the swap in person first, then make it official in the system.

## The Approval Process

A shift swap requires two approvals before it goes through:

1. **Your coworker** must accept the swap. They will see the request in their Shift Swaps tab and can accept or decline it.
2. **Your manager** must approve the swap. Once your coworker accepts, the request goes to your manager for final approval.

If either your coworker declines or your manager denies the request, the swap does not happen and both schedules remain unchanged.

## What Happens When a Swap Is Approved

Once both your coworker and your manager have approved the swap:

- Both schedules are **automatically updated** to reflect the new shifts.
- You will see the swapped shift on your **Calendar** in the Scheduling module.
- Your coworker will see their new shift as well.
- Both of you will receive a **notification** confirming the change.

No further action is needed from either person. The system handles the schedule changes.

> Warning: You are responsible for showing up to your new shift after a swap is approved. Forgetting about an approved swap does not excuse an absence.
