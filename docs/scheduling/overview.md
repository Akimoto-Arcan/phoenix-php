# Scheduling — Overview

The **Scheduling** module is where you view work schedules, request time off, and arrange shift swaps with coworkers. It replaces paper schedules and makes it easy to see who is working when.

## Main Areas

The Scheduling module has three main areas:

- **Calendar** — Shows the work schedule for the current week or month. You can see your own shifts or, if you are a manager, the shifts for your entire team.
- **Time Off** — Where you request vacation days, sick time, and other types of leave. You can also check the status of requests you have already submitted.
- **Shift Swaps** — Where you can trade shifts with a coworker. Both the coworker and a manager must approve the swap before it takes effect.

## Who Can See What

What you see in the Scheduling module depends on your role:

- **Employees** see their own schedule and can submit time off requests and shift swap requests.
- **Supervisors** see the schedules for everyone on their team. They can approve or deny time off requests and shift swaps.
- **Administrators** see all schedules across the entire facility and can make changes to any schedule.

> Tip: Check your schedule at the beginning of each week so you know your shifts ahead of time. Changes can happen, and the system always shows the most up-to-date version.
