# Low Stock Alerts

## What Is a Reorder Point?

A **reorder point** is the minimum stock level you set for an item. When the stock count drops to this number or below, the system flags the item as low stock. This is your signal that it is time to order more.

For example, if you set the reorder point to 10 and your stock drops to 8, the item will appear on the Low Stock page.

## How to Set the Reorder Point for an Item

1. Click **Inventory** in the main menu.
2. Click **Items**.
3. Find the item and click on it to open its detail page.
4. Click **Edit**.
5. Enter a number in the **Reorder Point** field.
6. Click **Save**.

> Tip: Set the reorder point based on how fast the item sells and how long it takes your supplier to deliver. If it takes two weeks to get a new shipment and you sell 5 per day, a reorder point of 70 or higher gives you a safe buffer.

## How to See Which Items Are Low

1. Click **Inventory** in the main menu.
2. Click **Low Stock**.

This page shows only items whose current stock count is at or below their reorder point. Items that are critically low are **highlighted in red** so they stand out.

The list shows each item's name, SKU, current stock count, and reorder point. This makes it easy to see how far below the minimum each item has fallen.

## What to Do When You See a Low Stock Alert

When an item appears on the Low Stock page, you have two options:

1. **Create a Purchase Order** — Go to **Purchase Orders** and create a new order for that item from the appropriate supplier.
2. **Receive Stock** — If stock has already arrived but has not been recorded, go to **Transactions** and use **Receive Stock** to update the count.

Once the stock count rises above the reorder point, the item automatically disappears from the Low Stock page.

> Warning: Items with no reorder point set will never appear on the Low Stock page, even if their stock reaches zero. Make sure to set a reorder point for every item you want to monitor.
