# Receiving Stock

## What Does Receiving Stock Mean?

**Receiving stock** means adding items to your inventory when they physically arrive. When a shipment comes in from a supplier, you record it in the system so your stock counts stay accurate.

Every time you receive stock, the system creates a **transaction record** so you have a complete history of what came in, when, and from whom.

## How to Receive Stock

1. Click **Inventory** in the main menu.
2. Click **Transactions**.
3. Click the **Receive Stock** button.
4. In the **Item** field, search for and select the item you are receiving.
5. Enter the **Quantity** — the number of units that arrived.
6. Click **Save** to complete the transaction.

The stock count for that item increases immediately.

## Optional Fields

You can fill in these additional fields to keep more detailed records:

- **PO Number** — If this shipment is tied to a purchase order, enter the PO number here. This links the receiving transaction to the original order.
- **Supplier** — Select the supplier who sent the shipment. This is helpful when you buy the same item from multiple vendors.
- **Cost Per Unit** — The price you paid per unit in this shipment. If the cost has changed since the last order, entering it here keeps your cost records up to date.

> Tip: Even though these fields are optional, filling in the PO Number makes it much easier to match shipments to purchase orders later.

## What Happens After You Save

- The item's **stock count** goes up by the quantity you entered.
- A new **transaction** is recorded with the date, time, item, quantity, and any optional details you provided.
- You can view this transaction at any time by going to **Transactions** and filtering by item or date.

> Tip: If you receive a shipment with multiple different items, you will need to create a separate receiving transaction for each item.
