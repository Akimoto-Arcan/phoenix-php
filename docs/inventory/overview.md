# Inventory Management — Overview

## What Is Inventory Management?

**Inventory management** is how you keep track of every product your business has in stock. It tells you what you have, how much of it you have, and when you need to order more.

Think of it as a digital stockroom ledger. Instead of counting items by hand or using spreadsheets, the system does the tracking for you automatically.

## Main Areas

Inventory management is divided into five sections. You can access each one from the **Inventory** menu.

- **Items** — Your master list of every product you track. Each item has a name, a price, a stock count, and other details.
- **Low Stock Alerts** — A filtered view that shows only items running low. These are items that have fallen below the minimum quantity you set.
- **Transactions** — A record of every time stock was added or removed. This includes receiving shipments, shipping orders, and adjustments.
- **Purchase Orders** — Orders you create when you need to buy more stock from a supplier.
- **Suppliers** — A directory of the vendors and companies you buy from.

## How Inventory Connects to POS

When a customer buys something through the **POS (Point of Sale)** system, the sold quantity is automatically subtracted from your inventory. You do not need to update stock counts manually after a sale.

For example, if you have 50 units of an item and a customer buys 3, the system updates the count to 47 on its own.

## Who Can Use It

The following **roles** have access to inventory management:

- **Admin** and **SuperAdmin** — Full access to all inventory features, including adding items, adjusting stock, and managing suppliers.
- **Supervisor** — Can view inventory, receive stock, and create purchase orders.
- **Operator** — Can view items and stock levels but cannot make changes.

> Tip: If you cannot see the Inventory menu, ask your administrator to check your role and permissions.
