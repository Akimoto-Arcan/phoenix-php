# Adding Inventory Items

## What Is an Inventory Item?

An **inventory item** is any product your business tracks. It could be a raw material, a finished good, a part, or a supply. Each item gets its own entry in the system with details like price, stock count, and supplier.

## How to Add a New Item

1. Click **Inventory** in the main menu.
2. Click **Items** to open the items list.
3. Click the **Add Item** button near the top of the page.
4. Fill in the item form (see the field descriptions below).
5. Click **Save** to create the item.

After you save, the new item appears in the items list with a stock count of **0**. You will need to receive stock separately to give it a quantity.

## Fields Explained

Here is what each field on the form means:

- **SKU** — Short for "Stock Keeping Unit." This is a unique code you assign to identify the item. It can be letters, numbers, or both (for example, "WDG-1042"). No two items can share the same SKU.
- **UPC** — The barcode number printed on the product packaging. If the item has a barcode, enter it here so the system can recognize it during scanning.
- **Name** — The item name as you want it to appear throughout the system. Use a clear, descriptive name (for example, "Blue Widget - Large").
- **Description** — An optional longer description with any extra details about the item.
- **Category** — The group this item belongs to (for example, "Raw Materials" or "Finished Goods"). Categories help you filter and organize your items list.
- **Unit of Measure** — How the item is counted. Common options include "Each," "Box," "Roll," "Pound," or "Gallon."
- **Cost Price** — How much you pay to buy one unit of this item from your supplier.
- **Sell Price** — How much you charge a customer for one unit. This is the price used in POS. If you leave this blank, the item will not appear in POS.
- **Reorder Point** — The minimum stock level that triggers a low stock alert. When your stock count drops to this number or below, the item shows up on the **Low Stock** page.
- **Supplier** — The vendor you buy this item from. Select from your existing supplier list.
- **Location** — Where the item is physically stored (for example, "Warehouse A, Shelf 3").

> Tip: At a minimum, fill in the SKU, Name, and Unit of Measure. The other fields are optional but recommended.

## How to Edit an Existing Item

1. Click **Inventory** in the main menu, then click **Items**.
2. Find the item you want to change. You can use the search bar to search by name or SKU.
3. Click the item row to open its detail page.
4. Click the **Edit** button.
5. Make your changes and click **Save**.

> Warning: Changing the SKU of an existing item may affect reports and transaction history. Only change it if absolutely necessary.
