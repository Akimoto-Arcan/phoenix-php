# Shipping Stock

## What Does Shipping Stock Mean?

**Shipping stock** means removing items from your inventory when they leave your facility. This applies to manual shipments, transfers to another location, or any situation where stock goes out for reasons other than a POS sale.

Each shipment is recorded as a **transaction** so you always have a record of what left and when.

## How to Ship Stock

1. Click **Inventory** in the main menu.
2. Click **Transactions**.
3. Click the **Ship Stock** button.
4. In the **Item** field, search for and select the item you are shipping.
5. Enter the **Quantity** — the number of units being shipped out.
6. Click **Save** to complete the transaction.

The stock count for that item decreases immediately.

## Stock Availability Check

Before completing the shipment, the system checks whether you have enough stock on hand. You cannot ship more than what is currently available.

If you try to ship more than your current stock count, you will see an error message like:

**"Insufficient stock. Available: 12. Requested: 25."**

To fix this, either reduce the quantity to match what is available, or receive more stock first.

## When You Need to Ship Manually

In most day-to-day operations, you do not need to use the Ship Stock feature because **POS sales deduct stock automatically**. The POS system handles the subtraction for you whenever a customer completes a purchase.

Use Ship Stock manually only when items are leaving for reasons other than a sale:

- Transferring stock to another warehouse or location
- Returning defective items to a supplier
- Writing off damaged or expired goods
- Fulfilling orders placed outside the POS system

> Tip: If your stock count seems too high, check whether manual shipments were missed for any outgoing orders.

> Warning: Shipping stock cannot be undone with a single click. If you ship the wrong quantity, you will need to create a new receiving transaction to add the stock back.
